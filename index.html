The game

A whole world to supply

Every map is made by hand: continents, mountain ranges, rivers, lakes and island chains. Towns and cities grow as you feed them, and some grow into each other.

How it plays

Land, logistics, production

Three ideas drive the game, and the roadmap below follows them.

Land

Buy your ground

The map is a patchwork of plots of different sizes. Buy a plot, build on it, then buy the one next door to grow.

Logistics

Run your fleet

Trucks, trains, boats, container ships and planes. Upgrade vehicles, add capacity and open routes that suit the cargo.

Production

Plan the chain

Raw goods become products through chains of buildings. Watch the rates, find the slow link and fix it.

Towns

Towns that grow

Towns and cities grow into their land. Neighbours can join up and share growth without swallowing each other.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

The early build in your browser is free to play while the game is in development.

Will there be a Windows version?

Yes. Use the Download for Windows button at the top. It updates itself in the background and keeps your place. The browser version will stay.

Will it come to Steam?

That is the plan. The Steam button will switch on once there is a page to link to.

Will my save carry over between builds?

Not always yet. The game changes quickly, so an early save may stop working after an update. Save versioning is on the list for later.

Does it run on a phone?

It is built for a desktop browser first. Phones can load it, but it is not tuned for them yet.
