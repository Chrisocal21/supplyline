The game

A whole world to supply

Every map is made by hand: continents, mountain ranges, rivers, lakes, deserts and island chains. Towns and cities grow as you feed them, and some grow into each other. You start with a Forestry Camp and a town that is still waiting for its Supply Yard.

Maps

Three worlds to play

Pick a map when you start a game. Each one is shown flat below; in the game it wraps round a globe.

Map 3: The Meridian Sea Beta

The big one. A great crescent continent round an inland sea, a cold northern land, belts of islands, a desert continent in the east and a dry belt in the south, with sea channels cut through the narrow necks of land so ships can reach more coast.

  • 768 x 352 tiles
  • about 5 times the land of Map 2
  • about 190 towns and cities
  • desert and arid towns
  • 22 sea channels

Map 2: The Continental

Four differently shaped continents and island chains, mountain ranges, a great river system running to a gulf, and about 46% sea. Coffee, mangoes and some oil lie overseas, so ports and planes matter.

  • 384 x 128 tiles
  • 55 towns and cities
  • rivers, lakes and a gulf

Map 1: The Known World

A hand-made world map of continents, islands and seas, with coffee, mango and oil islands to ship from. The best place to start.

  • 288 x 124 tiles
  • 33 towns and cities
  • island goods by ship

On the flat maps: white squares are towns, gold squares are cities, and the small dots are coffee (brown), mangoes (orange) and oil (black).

Start

A tutorial that steps aside

A short tutorial card walks you through the first town: the camera, a Forestry Camp, a road, the Supply Yard and the Market. It sits apart from the quests and switches off in the menu.

Build

Logs, then lumber

Your first town is built from raw logs. From Tier 1 it needs sawn lumber, then stone, bricks, steel and concrete as it grows. Green and orange ground shows where each building can go.

Grow

Towns that want more

A town grows from a village to a metropolis. Each level asks for a wider range of goods at its Market and heavier materials at its Supply Yard.

Run

Fleets, drivers and mechanics

Named drivers rank up, trucks wear out, and a Mechanic Shop with hired mechanics keeps the fleet on the road. Wages and upkeep are part of the bill.

Think

Production chains

Grain to flour to bread; clay to bricks; ore and coal to steel. Every factory shows its rates and what holds it up, and by-products have a buyer too.

Look

Seasons and a living world

Leaves turn, snow settles on the high ground, towns spread over their land and woods thin out as they do. Graphics presets suit a laptop or a big screen.

How it plays

Land, logistics, production

Three ideas drive the game, and the roadmap below follows them.

Land

Buy your ground

The map is a patchwork of plots of different sizes. Buy a plot, build on it, then buy the one next door to grow.

Logistics

Run your fleet

Trucks, trains, boats, container ships and planes. Upgrade vehicles, add capacity and open routes that suit the cargo.

Production

Plan the chain

Raw goods become products through chains of buildings. Watch the rates, find the slow link and fix it.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

The early build in your browser is free to play while the game is in development.

Which map should I start on?

Map 1 is the easiest to learn. Map 2 has more room and a great river system. Map 3 is the biggest and is in beta: it loads slowly the first time and wants a decent graphics card.

Will there be a Windows version?

Yes. Use the Download for Windows button at the top. It updates itself in the background and keeps your place. The browser version will stay.

Will it come to Steam?

That is the plan. The Steam button will switch on once there is a page to link to.

Will my save carry over between builds?

Mostly, but not always yet. If Map 3's land is edited, saves made before the edit cannot load, so the layout will be frozen as soon as testing allows. Exporting a save to a file from the menu always works.

Does it run on a phone?

It is built for a desktop browser first. Phones can load it, but it is not tuned for them yet.
