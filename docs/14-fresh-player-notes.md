## Moments to record exactly

- First sale: minute, and what they said
- First problem they solved alone
- First time they asked "what do I do now?"
- Any button or number they misread
- Frame-rate stutters or long pauses (when, doing what)
- When they wanted to stop, if they did

## Afterwards

Ask, and write their words:
1. What is the game about?
2. What was the most confusing moment?
3. What did you enjoy most?
4. Would you keep playing? Why or why not?
