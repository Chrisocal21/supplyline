About this game

Towns need goods. You grow the food, cut the wood and dig the stone, then deliver it. Fill the towns' shelves and a village becomes a town, a city, a metropolis.

  • A 3D globe with three hand-made maps, from a small teaching world to a huge one with about 190 towns.
  • 89 goods and 87 buildings, from raw logs to steel and machinery.
  • Move goods by truck, train, ship and plane, with roads, rails, bridges and canals.
  • Named drivers who rank up, and mechanics who keep the fleet running.
  • Five tiers of goals, with a guide for your first town and a relaxed mode with no punishment.

We are looking for 3D artists

The game you see today uses stand-in models while the rules and the world are built. A full overhaul of the artwork is coming, and we want artists to help make it look right.

  • Buildings, vehicles, town pieces and props, low to mid poly, built for a world seen from far out and from the street.
  • A consistent look across hundreds of pieces.
